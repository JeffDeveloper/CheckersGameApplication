Checkers

Two players, one screen, rules enforced automatically.

How to play
  • Move one square diagonally, forward only.
  • Jump an adjacent opposing piece to capture it — captures are mandatory, and chain if more are open.
  • Reach the far row to crown a king, which moves in any diagonal direction.
  • Win by leaving your opponent with no pieces or no legal move.
